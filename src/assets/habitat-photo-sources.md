## Rio index scope

The index excludes Campinarana, Campo de Várzea, Floresta de Igapó,
Floresta de Terra Firme, Floresta de Várzea, Floresta Estacional Perenifólia
and Savana Amazônica. The [Jardim Botânico vocabulary](https://dadoswiki.jbrj.gov.br/doku.php?id=vocabulario:vegetacao)
describes these national categories as Amazonian, including the southern Amazon
for the seasonal evergreen forest. The catalog's original habitat fields remain
unchanged. Other categories are retained where occurrence needs further review;
species-level habitat associations alone do not establish local occurrence.
